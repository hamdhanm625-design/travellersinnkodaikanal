About Travellers Inn Tours & Travels

Travellers Inn Tours & Travels is a trusted travel agency based in Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu, specializing in airport transfers, sightseeing packages, family tours, honeymoon travel, and customized hill itineraries across the Western Ghats.

Led by owner Sulthan Ibrahim, the company is known for punctual service, safe mountain driving, transparent pricing, and personalized plans for couples, families, and groups.

We serve travellers arriving from Madurai, Coimbatore, Dindigul, Trichy, Chennai and nearby destinations, helping them move smoothly between hill stations, airports, railway stations, and key attractions.
